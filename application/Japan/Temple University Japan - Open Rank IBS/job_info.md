## Role focus
Undergraduate International Business Studies (IBS), TUJ’s largest major. Connect business/management principles to AI, geopolitics, global value chains, and corporate responsibility. English-medium teaching. Desired start **July 2027**. Fixed-term contracts; indefinite contract possible after five years and a positive fourth-year review. Visa sponsorship available. Professorial load typically six courses/year (2–2–2); Lecturer/Senior Lecturer typically ten courses/year. Graduate degree (MBA, master’s, DBA, Ph.D., or other terminal) in international business, business administration, management, economics, or closely related field. Required: documented undergraduate instructor-of-record experience (or clearly documented substantial co-teaching).
