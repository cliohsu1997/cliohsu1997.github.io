業務内容

  • 募集の背景、プロジェクトの説明

    We seek a dynamic and passionate educator in the field of Economics and/or Finance to join our interdisciplinary liberal arts program. The ideal candidate will have expertise in Macroeconomics and Econometrics. The ability to also teach in the area of Finance, such as International Finance, is highly desirable.

  • 仕事内容・職務内容

    The successful candidate will be expected to teach a range of foundational and advanced undergraduate courses such as:
    - Economics: Macroeconomics, Intermediate Macroeconomics, Econometrics
    - Finance: International Finance, Risk Management, Portfolio Management
    - Other possible courses: Related subjects in Economics and Business

    Preference will be given to candidates who can teach across both Economics and Finance, who demonstrate a capacity to develop innovative, interdisciplinary courses tailored to a liberal arts context, and who can contribute to the Global Business and Economics program.

    The successful candidate will also be expected to mentor students, contribute to curriculum development, and engage in service to the college.
