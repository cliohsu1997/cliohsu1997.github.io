## Role focus
Start September 2027. Assistant Professor: Ph.D. in hand or firmly expected by August 2027, and promise of academic and teaching ability. Associate Professor: an excellent research and teaching record. All fields are welcome. Preferred fields are financial economics, international economics, public finance, economic history, development economics, and risk management and insurance. Keywords also include AI and quantitative economics and computational economics.
