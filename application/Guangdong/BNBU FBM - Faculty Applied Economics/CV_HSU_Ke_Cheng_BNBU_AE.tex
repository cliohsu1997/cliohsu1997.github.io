\sectionheader{WORKING PAPERS}
\begin{itemize}
  \item ``Designing Return Policy in E-Commerce: A Trade-off between Purchase and Return Efficiency'' \textit{(Job Market Paper)}\\[0.15em]
  \textit{Abstract:} We study whether return policies encourage efficient returns and purchases. A stricter policy curbs inefficient ex-post returns, but lower return rates make higher prices more attractive for sellers and can exclude efficient purchases. Using Taobao apparel data, we estimate a structural model of purchase, insurance, and return under monopoly pricing across six categories. Automatic return-shipping coverage performs better in high-margin categories; optional coverage performs better in thinner-margin categories. A menu pairing a discounted price with a medium restocking fee balances both margins for most categories.\\[0.1em]
  \textit{Presented at ($^{\ast}$ scheduled):} APIOC 2026$^{\ast}$; ESAM 2026$^{\ast}$; 中國經濟學年會夏季論壇 2026; HKUST Business School PhD Conference; CEP Brownbag in HKUST.

  \item ``Product Design with Heterogeneous Search Costs'', with Pak Hung AU\\[0.15em]
  \textit{Abstract:} We introduce search-cost heterogeneity into a sequential-search model with differentiated products. In equilibrium, low-search-cost consumers seek niche products and high-search-cost consumers seek standardized products. A fall in search costs can raise prices when the composition effect dominates.\\[0.1em]
  \textit{Presented at:} APIOC 2023; AMES 2023 NTU Singapore.
\end{itemize}
