## Role focus (from the posting)

Contribute to BIS economic research and analysis with economists and research analysts in Basel. Policy-oriented research supporting central banks and financial authorities.

**Topics include:**

- Financial intermediation and regulation

- Monetary policy
- Fiscal policy
- International finance and international spillovers

**By joining you will:**

- Contribute to BIS research projects on these topics
- Be embedded in MED with regular staff interaction
- Access BIS seminars and visiting research fellows

- Enrolment in a PhD programme (preferably 3rd–5th year) in economics, finance, or related fields
- Expertise in research in financial, macro, and monetary economics
- Ability to apply tools typically used in economic analysis (e.g.\ Python, MATLAB, Stata, EViews) across topics
- Familiarity with major financial/monetary databases beneficial
