## Role focus
High-level talent hire at SZU College of Economics. Rank expectations:

1. **Professor** — prior assoc/full (or overseas teaching post); cutting-edge research; independent academic direction; top of age cohort.
2. **Associate Professor** — ≥2 years research experience at well-known institutions; outstanding research; top of age cohort.
3. **Assistant Professor** — strong research / innovation / teamwork; influential publications; top of age cohort. **Top-tier papers required at application**; otherwise consider the 2-year postdoc + college-level assistant professor track.

**Age:** generally ≤35 (≤37 for female applicants); humanities/social sciences may relax up to **40**. Full-time on campus; doctoral degree from a renowned university/institution.

**Package (within 6-year Hundred Talents period):** assistant ≥ RMB 600k; associate ≥ RMB 690k; full ≥ RMB 830k comprehensive annual salary; plus Pengcheng Peacock Class-C allowance RMB 240k/year if eligible; Shenzhen new-PhD living subsidy RMB 100k if eligible. Start-up research funds RMB 1M–3.3M; office/lab; master’s admission quota if PhD supervisor; team support (one full-time researcher). Preferential university housing; Shenzhen talent housing / Guangdong & Pengcheng talent cards; affiliated schools and hospitals.
