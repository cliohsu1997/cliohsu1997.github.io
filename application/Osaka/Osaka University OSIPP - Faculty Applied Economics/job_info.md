## Role focus
Start **2027-04-01**. Research in applied economics. Teach micro, macro, or econometrics (and related), graduate courses in the applicant’s field, thesis supervision, undergraduate lectures/seminars, plus OSIPP / university admin. English teaching required; Japanese preferred for teaching and at least for administrative communication (read/write/speak). Tenure-track: 4-year term with tenure review; possible one reappointment up to 3 years. Teaching load lower until first tenure review (Lecturer ~2 courses/year; TT Assoc ~3). Startup research funds for new hires. Probation 6 months.

**Tenure-track eligibility (relevant for JM):** Ph.D. within the past 10 years or expected (must pass degree exam by day before start); papers that demonstrate research ability; English teaching.

**Tenured eligibility (harder for JM):** Ph.D. already awarded; ≥3 peer-reviewed papers in internationally recognized English journals (at least one sole-authored in principle).

## Applicant materials
All documents in English or Japanese.

### Tenure-track
1. CV including publication list (name with furigana, contact, DOB, education, employment) **plus ≥2 referees’ names and contacts** (phone and email)
2. If Ph.D. not yet awarded: advisor / thesis-chair letter confirming expected degree by day before start (may be inside a recommendation letter)
3. Up to **three** papers for review (PDF; published or unpublished)
4. **≥2 recommendation letters** — referees submit via JOE by the deadline
5. Optional: cover letter, teaching evaluations, other supporting materials

### Tenured
1. CV including publication list (highlight published / forthcoming peer-reviewed papers)
2. **Three** papers for review (PDF)
3. Optional: cover letter, teaching evaluations, other supporting materials

## Recommendation letters
**Tenure-track only:** at least **two** letters via **JOE** (writers submit after JOE contact). Not email-to-school — not added to the email-only `recommendation_letters_to_send` tracker.
