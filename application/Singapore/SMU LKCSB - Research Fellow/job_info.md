## Role focus
Support data work, empirical analysis, and literature synthesis on projects involving large-scale and unstructured data. Duties include collecting/cleaning/structuring large datasets (text, web pages, patents, app-usage records); reproducible code in Python, R, or Stata; advanced empirical models (DiD, IV, field experiments); literature reviews; project materials (presentations, reports, grants); organize events/conferences.

## Qualifications (verbatim from portal)
- PhD or Master’s in finance, economics, computer science, statistics, or a related quantitative field
- Strong programming in Python, R, or Stata; hands-on experience processing large databases
- Demonstrated experience with unstructured data (text, web, audio, or **image**)
- Solid training in empirical methods, especially causal inference; ML and **structural estimation** a plus
- Strong knowledge of and research background in finance or economics
- Excellent research skills; banking, fintech, or innovation research preferred
- Strong written and oral communication skills, including presentations

## Duties (verbatim from portal)
Collect, clean, structure large-scale data incl. unstructured (text, web pages, patents, app-usage); write efficient, well-documented, reproducible code in Python / R / Stata; design and implement advanced empirical models incl. causal inference (DiD, IV, field experiments); targeted literature reviews; prepare project materials (presentations, progress reports, research outputs, grant documentation); organize events (conferences); other PI-assigned tasks.
