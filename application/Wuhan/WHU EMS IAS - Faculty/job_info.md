# Wuhan University — Economics and Management School (EMS) & Institute for Advanced Study (IAS)

## Position
Full-time tenure-track / tenured positions at all levels (EMS and/or IAS)

## Location
Wuhan, Hubei, China

## Deadline
**2026-11-10** (guaranteed interviews before 2027 AEA meetings). Applications considered until positions are filled. JOE listing deadline **2026-11-10**.

## How to apply
Submit online via **JOE** only. Clearly indicate in the cover letter whether applying to the **Economics and Management School** or the **Institute for Advanced Study**.

## Role focus
Start Fall 2027. All fields; especially Insurance, Macroeconomics, Empirical Industrial Organization, Econometrics, and Finance. EMS assistant professor: typically a top field journal publication (more flexible for Insurance/Risk Management with strong pipeline). Junior candidates without publications encouraged to apply for postdoc. Associate/Full: excellent publication records. IAS tenured Assoc/Full: qualifications comparable to top-tier universities; special university support. Competitive compensation.

## Applicant materials
- Cover letter (state EMS vs IAS)
- CV
- Job market paper
- Research statement
- Question and Answer (JOE requirement)

## Recommendation letters
Three letters of recommendation (sent separately by referees via JOE). For tenured associate/full, letters may be arranged after shortlisting. Tracked in `application/recommendation_letters_to_send.xlsx` / `.pdf`.
