# Jinan University — Institute for Economic and Social Research (IESR)

## Position
International Research Fellow (3-year fixed-term; no extension)

## Location
Guangzhou, Guangdong, China

## Deadline
**2027-07-31** (JOE listing deadline)

## How to apply
Submit via **JOE**, or email materials to **iesr_job@126.com**. Clearly indicate applying for **“International Research Fellow”**.

## Role focus
Three-year fixed-term research fellowship for outstanding young international scholars. Preferred fields: Economics, AI, and Statistics (non-economists in AI/statistics should show interest in applying work to economics). On-site; independent research; attend IESR academic activities. PhD within the past three years or to be awarded before start. Competitive salary plus research and international travel funding.

## Applicant materials
- Cover letter
- CV
- Statement outlining the next 3-year research plans
- Copies of degree certificates and other supporting materials

## Recommendation letters
At least **two** recommendation letters — institute page asks materials (including letters) emailed to **iesr_job@126.com**. Tracked in `application/recommendation_letters_to_send.xlsx` / `.pdf`.
