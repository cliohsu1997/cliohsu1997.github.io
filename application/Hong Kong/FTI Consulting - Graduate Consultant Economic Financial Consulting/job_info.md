## Role focus (from the posting)

Economic Consulting practice (compact team): oral and written expert evidence on damages quantification, accounting issues, loss of profits, business/instrument valuation, and regulatory issues in commercial and investment-treaty disputes.

Day-to-day:

- Identify relevant project material and deliver supporting research
- Design and build valuation models; perform financial analysis
- Analyse large volumes of data; perform statistical analysis
- Assist with reports, written analyses, presentations, quantitative exhibits, and other client deliverables
- Investigate and critique assertions, analyses, and work product of other experts
- Assist in Asia-Pacific business development as needed

**Growth:** CPA or CFA sponsorship (course/exam/membership fees reimbursed; paid study/exam leave); two-week London training for new joiners (EMEA + APAC network).
