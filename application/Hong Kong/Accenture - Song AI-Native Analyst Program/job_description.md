## About Song

Song helps clients grow. By bringing together strategy, technology, AI and creativity, we help clients reinvent marketing, sales and service, and create better customer experiences.

From growth strategy and product experience design to brand marketing and customer operations, Song helps clients build end-to-end growth capabilities. You will work on innovation projects and explore how AI reshapes consumer experiences and business models.

## Join us if you

- Follow consumer and market trends
- Are interested in AI and digital experiences
- Think creatively and bring new ideas
- Want to drive growth through technology and creativity

## Requirements

- Bachelor’s degree or above; Full-time students graduating from domestic or overseas institutions between January 1, 2026 and August 31, 2027
- Majors from any discipline are welcome; AI, data analysis, business analysis, digital marketing, experience design, ecommerce, computer science, statistics, mathematics and engineering related majors and backgrounds are preferred
- Proficiency in English and Chinese (Cantonese & Mandarin)
- Digitally savvy, curious, and passionate about technology, with an interest in emerging technologies, business innovation, and the application of AI in work and daily life
- Strong AI literacy and critical thinking skills, with the ability to leverage AI to support learning, analysis, problem-solving, and value creation
- Strong quantitative problem-solving skills, with a desire to develop and deepen consulting capabilities
- Ability to work collaboratively with different people, effectively articulating ideas to help teams or workgroups achieve their goals
- Proven ability to adapt, overcome obstacles to achieve and deliver results, with the courage and flexibility to undertake work responsibilities in different projects/locations
