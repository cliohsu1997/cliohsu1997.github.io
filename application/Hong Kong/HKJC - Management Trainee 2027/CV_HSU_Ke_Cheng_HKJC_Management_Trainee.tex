{\large \textbf{Projects}}\\[-0.5pt]
\textbf{Return Policy Design in E-Commerce} \textit{(Job Market Paper)}\\
\vspace{-6pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Studied how lenient an online apparel seller's return policy should be, using data from Taobao (2017--2023; six apparel categories; $\sim$62M RMB revenue; 20--30\% return rates).
  \item Compared a lenient policy (seller pays return shipping) against a strict one (buyer pays), and proposed a third option: a menu letting buyers choose between a lower price with a partial restocking fee and a full price with a full refund.
  \item Found the menu beats both, reaching about 75\% of the best attainable outcome in most categories; a back-of-envelope calculation suggests about 5 million RMB in profit improvement. The same method answers what a product team asks before a launch: how much a new product would sell, what it would contribute to profit, and whether it would cannibalize existing products.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Inventory and Pricing in the Japanese Book Market} \textit{(with K.\ Kawaguchi, HKUST; K.\ Kawai, U.\ Tokyo)}\\
