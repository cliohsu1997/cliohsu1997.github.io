{\large \textbf{Projects}}\\[-0.5pt]
\textbf{Return Policy Design in E-Commerce} \textit{(Job Market Paper)}\\
\vspace{-6pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a structural model of purchase, return-shipping-insurance take-up, and return decisions under monopoly pricing, using within-retailer switches on Taobao (2017--2023; six apparel categories; $\sim$62M RMB revenue; $\sim$20--30\% return rates); scraped and OCR-digitized product attributes in Python.
  \item Estimated demand, consumer fit uncertainty, and return responses; simulated counterfactual policies and compared seller profit, consumer surplus, and social welfare with bootstrap inference.
  \item Found that a lenient policy (seller pays return shipping) suits high-margin categories, a strict policy (buyer pays) suits thin-margin ones (improving welfare by $\sim$50 pp in the thinnest), and a proposed menu (lower price with partial restocking fee vs. full price with full refund) beats both, reaching $\sim$75\% of the best attainable outcome in most categories; back-of-envelope calculation suggests $\sim$5M RMB profit improvement.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{-3pt}
\textbf{Inventory and Pricing under Resale Price Maintenance (RPM)} \textit{(w/ K.\ Kawaguchi, HKUST; K.\ Kawai, U.\ Tokyo)}\\
\vspace{-6pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a dynamic oligopoly model of pricing and inventory under demand uncertainty and RPM, with equilibrium stockouts and unsold returns (35--40\% of Japanese books printed are discarded); constructed panels from monthly bookstore POS data (tens of billions of records across 1{,}000+ stores), linked via ISBNs and merged with demographics and title-level information scraped from Amazon.
  \item Estimated demand and supply with instrumental variables; validated the estimator with Monte Carlo; implemented equilibrium simulation in C++.
  \item Found that the static model overestimates marginal cost by $\sim$20\% when ignoring dynamic demand uncertainty and inventory; uncovered stockout-driven substitution patterns relevant for inventory and assortment decisions.
\end{itemize}
