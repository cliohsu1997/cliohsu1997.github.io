\textbf{Pricing and Returns in Apparel E-Commerce} \textit{(Taobao, 2017--2023)}\\
\vspace{1pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a structural model of purchase, insurance, and return decisions under seller pricing for a Taobao apparel retailer that switched between automatic return-shipping insurance (seller-paid) and optional, buyer-purchased insurance (2017--2023; about 62 million RMB; six categories; 20--30\% of orders returned).
  \item Automated data extraction with a Python scraper and an API, converting product images into analyzable text.
  \item Estimated the model by the generalized method of moments in a tested pipeline (Git for version control; Python, R, and C++ on AWS and a SLURM cluster), obtaining category-specific purchase values, demand uncertainty, and return responses.
  \item A stricter return policy curbs wasteful returns but also blocks worthwhile purchases: automatic insurance works best at high margins, while optional insurance wins at thin margins, by 15--30 percentage points of the best-possible outcome (about 50 percentage points in the thinnest category).
  \item Designed a new what-if policy (discounted price plus a 10--15\% restocking fee) that beats both existing policies in most categories, attaining 75--78\% of the best-possible outcome.
\end{itemize}
