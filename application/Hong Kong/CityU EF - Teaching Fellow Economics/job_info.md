## Role focus
Full-time fixed-term teaching appointment.

Duties: teach economics and related disciplines at undergraduate and postgraduate levels, including evening classes; lead in the candidate's specialism; teaching-related work, learning support, and professional activities; supervise student projects, prepare study materials, and set and moderate examination papers; administrative duties as required by the Head of Department.

Requirements: a doctoral degree in Economics or a related discipline, with evidence of quality teaching. All areas of economics are welcome, with particular interest in **empirical economics**, **applied econometrics**, and **policies in the recent China economy**.

Rank bars:
- **Senior Teaching Fellow:** substantial professional experience and recognisable achievements in non-academic settings (prerequisite), plus a distinguished record of teaching and administration, instructional development, curriculum planning, and student supervision.
- **Teaching Fellow:** relevant professional experience and expertise in non-academic settings (prerequisite), with administrative experience.
- **Instructor I:** at least 4 years of appropriate postgraduate full-time teaching. Less experience may be considered for **Instructor II**.

Remuneration is competitive and commensurate with qualifications and experience. Appointment is on a fixed-term contract. Fringe benefits include gratuity, leave, and medical and dental schemes. Only shortlisted applicants are contacted.
