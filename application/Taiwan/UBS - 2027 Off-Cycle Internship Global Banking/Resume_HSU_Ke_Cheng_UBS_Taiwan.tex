{\large \textbf{Projects}}\\[1pt]
\textbf{Pricing and Inventory in Japanese Book Retail} \textit{(w/ K.\ Kawaguchi, HKUST; K.\ Kawai, U.\ Tokyo)}\\
\vspace{1pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a dynamic model of pricing and inventory for a retail oligopoly under resale price maintenance and uncertain demand, including stockouts and unsold returns (35--40\% of books printed are discarded).
  \item Constructed title-by-market-by-month panels from bookstore point-of-sale data (deliveries, sales, returns; tens of billions of records across 1{,}000+ stores), linked by ISBN and merged with local demographics and title-level information scraped from Amazon in Python.
  \item Estimated demand and cost with instrumental variables (2SLS) and Hausman-style price instruments; checked the estimator with Monte Carlo; selected controls with LASSO and panel fixed effects; coded the equilibrium iteration in C++. Ignoring inventory and uncertain demand overstates marginal cost by about 20\% and would misstate margins.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Pricing and Returns in Apparel E-Commerce} \textit{(Taobao, 2017--2023)}\\
\vspace{1pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Collected product attributes in Python: wrote a scraping library, connected to an API, and used OCR to convert product images into text.
  \item Cleaned raw Taobao exports of orders, products, delivery, insurance, and addresses into linked transaction tables (2017--2023; \(\sim\)62M RMB; six apparel categories; return rates \(\sim\)20--30\%): dropped non-products, giveaways, partial refunds, and orders missing logistics; separated retailer-provided from consumer-purchased insurance; standardized addresses to provinces.
  \item Estimated how purchases and returns respond to price and insurance via GMM (Python, R, C++ on AWS and HPC). Broader insurance expands purchases where margins are high; selective adoption protects seller profit where margins are thin by cutting returns.
\end{itemize}
