\textbf{Pricing and Returns in Apparel E-Commerce} \textit{(Taobao, 2017--2023)}\\
\vspace{1pt}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a structural model of purchase, insurance, and return decisions under seller pricing, for a Taobao apparel retailer that repeatedly switched between automatic return-shipping insurance and optional, buyer-purchased insurance (2017--2023; about 62 million RMB; six categories; 20--30\% of orders returned).
  \item Automated data extraction with a Python scraper, an API, and AI-based OCR, converting product images into analyzable text.
  \item Estimated the model by the generalized method of moments in a tested pipeline (Python, R, and C++ on AWS and a computing cluster), recovering category-specific purchase values, demand uncertainty, and return responses.
  \item A stricter return policy curbs wasteful returns but excludes worthwhile purchases: automatic insurance wins at high margins; optional insurance wins at thin margins, by 15--30 percentage points of first-best welfare.
  \item Presented at the Asia-Pacific Industrial Organization Conference 2026, the Econometric Society Australasia Meeting 2026, and the China Economics Annual Conference Summer Forum 2026.
\end{itemize}
