Abstract: Resale price maintenance is procompetitive because it encourages sellers to carry inventory in the low-demand state. When realized demand falls short of that inventory, however, the seller cannot lower the price to clear the market, and the unsold copies are returned at an environmental cost. We build a dynamic oligopoly model with demand uncertainty, estimate it on monthly bookstore data, and ask how much unsold stock is due to price commitment and how a deposit-refund tax can reduce waste.
